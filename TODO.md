# Parked items

Things we know about and will fix later. Each one also has a `TODO(parked)` comment at the place in the code where the fix goes.

| # | Item | Where | Branch |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Weekend spends don't follow the real calendar.** The mock history is moved by whole months to follow the clock. That keeps day-of-month patterns ("paid on the 5th") but not weekdays. So "Dining out is up, mostly on weekends" and "Show weekends" can contradict the calendar. On Saturday 7 Nov 2026 the claim rests on one weekend day (Sunday 1 Nov), and in other months the "Saturdays" land on weekdays. Fix: place dining spends on the real Saturdays and Sundays of each shifted month, and only claim "mostly on weekends" once a full weekend has passed. | `lib/data/mock_bank.dart` (Priya's food spends), `lib/engine/insights.dart` (`spendSummary` insight) | both |
| 2 | **Live voice is slow to connect and sometimes fails.** "AI · live" can take 15–30 s after sign-in, one start gave a connection error, and until it connects the mic quietly uses the device recogniser, which never speaks. Ideas: connect during the sign-in checklist, retry once with a fresh key, keep the fallback message visible, and time each connection step. | `lib/speech/realtime_voice.dart` (`connect`) | `feature/realtime-voice` |
| 3 | **The on-screen transcript is English-only for now.** Live voice shows what it heard using `whisper-1`, set to the customer's first language (`spokenLanguages.first`, English for Priya), because left to guess it once wrote Indian-accented English in Tamil script. So Tamil or Hindi speech appears as English text; the voice model still understands it, since it hears the audio itself. `whisper-1` also has a shutdown announced for 26 Feb 2027. **Fix:** switch to `gpt-live-transcribe` with `languages: profile.spokenLanguages` (Priya: en, ta, hi) and `keywords:` the payee names, so the transcript follows the language spoken. That suits the European customers too. **Status:** `gpt-live-transcribe` is deployed on `1000034329-fsto-dev1-resource` (Global Standard, East US 2, version 2026-07-28, 15K TPM), and the realtime session accepts these settings. But on 12 Nov 2026, 8 test runs gave 5 correct transcripts, 2 failures with "OperationNotSupported", and "Pay Airtel" once came back as "Airtel." Without keywords it heard "Paytel". Retest before switching; it may have been settling in after deployment. Also check data residency: Global Standard may process data outside the region. | `lib/engine/voice_agent.dart` (`transcription`) | `feature/realtime-voice` |
